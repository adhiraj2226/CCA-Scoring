CCA Scoring

Six activities a year · each scored 0 to 4 · medals worked out automatically

Add many students at once

One student per line. You can paste a column of names straight from Excel or Google Sheets. To add scores too, write: Name, then six scores (example: Asha, 4, 3, 2, 1, 4, 2).

Activity medal: 4 gold · 3 silver · 2 green · 1 red. Overall (sum of six): 15 and above gold · 10 to 14 silver · 5 to 9 green · 1 to 4 red. Data is saved in this browser only.
